{# Hero backdrop image. AVIF is offered first and WebP is the fallback: a browser that cannot decode AVIF skips the and uses the src instead. That negotiation is the whole reason this is a rather than a CSS background — it is the one mechanism every engine implements, including ones too old to understand `image-set()` type hints. `alt=""` is deliberate. The picture carries no information the surrounding copy does not; announcing it would add noise to a screen reader, not meaning. Its wrapper is `aria-hidden` for the same reason. Callers may set, before including: hero_loading 'eager' (default) or 'lazy' hero_priority 'high' (default) or 'auto' Use 'lazy'/'auto' where the container can be hidden by a media query — the browser then skips the download entirely instead of spending ~200KB on a panel the visitor will never see. #}
